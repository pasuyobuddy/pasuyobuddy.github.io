Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Massage Buddy respects your privacy and follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

Customers:

  • Name, mobile number and email address
  • Service address and city, and notes you give the therapist
  • Booking history, reviews and messages you send us
  • Payment records (amount, status, reference). Card details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store your full card number.

Therapists:

  • Name, mobile number, email, gender, service area, profile photos and bio
  • Verification documents: government ID, selfie with ID, NBI Clearance, Barangay Clearance, certificates
  • Schedule, bookings, earnings and payout details

Automatically: basic device and usage information needed to run the app safely (for example login times and, if you allow it, a notification token so we can send booking alerts).

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you log in
  • To make, manage and remind you about bookings, and share the needed details between customer and therapist (e.g. the therapist sees your address and number only for your booking)
  • To process payments, refunds and therapist payouts
  • To verify therapists and keep customers and therapists safe
  • To handle complaints, prevent fraud and fake bookings, and follow the law
  • To improve our service

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

  • The other side of your booking (customer or therapist), only what is needed for the booking
  • Service providers that help us run Massage Buddy: secure cloud hosting and database, payment processor (e.g. PayMongo), email and push-notification services. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety (for example in a theft or assault case)

Some of our providers may store data outside the Philippines. We use providers with appropriate security safeguards.

4. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure login
  • Database access rules so each person sees only their own data
  • Therapist ID and clearance documents are kept private and can only be viewed by authorised Massage Buddy staff

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for bookings, payments, safety, disputes and legal or tax requirements. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use these rights, or to delete your account, email pasuyolabs@gmail.com from the email linked to your account. We will reply within a reasonable time. Some records (e.g. payment records) may need to be kept for legal reasons.

7. Children

Massage Buddy is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its date.

9. Data Protection Officer

For privacy questions or concerns, contact our Data Protection Officer at pasuyolabs@gmail.com.
