Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

We want bookings to be fair for both customers and therapists. Therapists travel through traffic to reach you, so late cancellations and no-shows have a fee. This policy is part of our Terms & Conditions.

1. If the customer cancels

When you cancelFeeRefund to you
Before the therapist accepts the bookingNone100%
After the therapist accepts, before they are on the way20% of the total80%
Therapist is already on the way or at your door50% of the total50%
No-show: you are not at the address or do not answer for 15 minutes after the booking time50% of the total50%

Cancellation fees mainly compensate the therapist for their time and travel.

2. If the therapist cancels, is late or does not arrive

  • Therapist cancels: you get a 100% refund.
  • Therapist is more than 30 minutes late: you may cancel free with a 100% refund.
  • Therapist does not arrive: tap "Therapist didn't arrive" in the app. You get a 100% refund, and the therapist is suspended while we review.

3. Problems during or after the massage

  • If the service was not as booked (e.g. wrong massage type or much shorter time), report it in the app within 24 hours. We will review it and may give a full or partial refund.
  • For serious issues (theft, misconduct, safety), use "Report a problem" or email us right away. We will act immediately.
  • Completed massages are not refundable just because of personal preference (e.g. you wanted a different pressure). Please tell your therapist during the massage so they can adjust.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to your original payment method (GCash, Maya or card).
  • Most refunds are processed within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or e-wallet.
  • Payment provider fees on refunded payments may be non-refundable where the provider does not return them.

5. Cash bookings

Where cash payment is allowed, cancellation and no-show fees are added to your account and must be paid before your next booking.

6. Repeated cancellations

To stop fake bookings, accounts with many late cancellations or no-shows may be blocked from booking.

7. Questions

Email pasuyolabs@gmail.com with your booking number. We reply as soon as possible.
