Delete your PasuyoBuddy account

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Customers: open the PasuyoBuddy app → Account → Delete my account (at the bottom).
  2. Riders: open PasuyoBuddy Rider → Account → Delete my account. Cash out your earnings first.
  3. Tick the box to confirm and tap Delete my account. Your account is switched off right away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email pasuyolabs@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". Tell us if you are a customer or a rider. We reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and login
  • Your profile photo, ID photos, selfie and rider documents (license, OR/CR, NBI, letter of intent)
  • Saved addresses, notification settings and rider payout details

We erase these within 30 days of your request.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Booking, payment, wallet and commission records are kept without your personal details for up to 10 years, because Philippine tax and accounting laws require it.
  • Records needed for an open complaint, dispute or legal case are kept until it is settled.

Money in your account

  • Customers: any PasuyoBuddy Wallet balance is lost when the account is deleted. Use it first.
  • Riders: cash out your earnings before deleting. Top-up load is non-refundable and is removed.

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. PasuyoBuddy follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
