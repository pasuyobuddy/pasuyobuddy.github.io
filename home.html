What can we do for you?

One app for everyday deliveries, for people and for online sellers.

📦

Pasuyo Delivery

Pick up from one place and deliver to up to 4 drop-offs in one booking. Great for documents, parcels, food and online orders.

Tell us what to buy. The rider pays at the store first, then delivers to you. You pay the items plus the delivery fee on arrival.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

For online sellers: the rider collects the item price from your buyer, so you get paid without the hassle.

Questions

How do I pay?

Pay cash to the rider. The exact amount is shown before you book. More payment options are coming soon.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You can book right here in your browser on your phone or computer. An Android app is also available.

Can the receiver pay the delivery fee?

Yes. For each drop-off you can choose whether you or the receiver pays the fare.

Can I cancel?

Yes, you can cancel in the app before the rider picks up your item.

What items are not allowed?

Illegal or dangerous items, weapons, live animals and cash for remittance. See the full list in our Terms & Conditions.
