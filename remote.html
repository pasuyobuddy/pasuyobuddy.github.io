How it works: open this page on both devices. On the computer, press Start sharing and pick a screen or window. On your phone/laptop, enter the 6‑letter code (or scan the QR). The computer must press Allow before anyone can see anything. Video goes directly between the two devices, encrypted (WebRTC).
